Personal Statement & Mission

Medhavi Vyas

I am a Rutgers Business School student with internship experience at Schindler Elevator and Withum, focusing on finance and corporate accounting.

As a Finance and Accounting junior at Rutgers Business School, I have built a rigorous quantitative foundation through coursework in Intermediate Accounting, Corporate Finance, Income Tax, International Financial Management, and Investment Analysis. I am passionate about leveraging technical accounting principles and data-driven financial analysis to solve complex business challenges in professional corporate settings.

During my sophomore year as a Finance Controller Intern at Schindler Elevator Corporation, I collaborated with executive teams to author a comprehensive training handbook for a global SAP transition, ensuring operational documentation standards. Additionally, I assisted with monthly and quarterly closing cycles, consolidating key financial data for executive presentation and CFO reporting.

Continuing my professional development at Withum as an Administrative Intern, I managed large-scale tax documentation workflows, optimized multi-step verification systems, and maintained client IRS correspondence to support seamless tax operations during peak business periods.
